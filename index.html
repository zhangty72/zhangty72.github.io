Tim Zhang · Ph.D. Candidate

I study how phase-change droplets and nonlinear dynamics shape ultrasound for biomedical sensing.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Michigan, working with Prof. Chengzhi Shi in the Meta Acoustic Lab. My research connects acoustic radiation, droplet activation, and nonlinear control to develop stronger and more reproducible ultrasound readouts. I combine experiments with physical models, building on earlier work in soft acoustic metamaterials and bioinspired hydrogels.

Acoustic phase control of internal pressure and nucleation exposure in resonant liquid microdroplets

Tianye Zhang, Ge Wang, Chenzhe Wang, Jianyu Hua, and Chengzhi Shi

Submitted to The Journal of the Acoustical Society of America

A theoretical and computational study of how auxiliary phase and delay tune internal droplet pressure and a modeled nucleation-exposure measure. It provides a framework for selecting waveforms relevant to phase-change ultrasound agents; physical vaporization outcomes remain to be tested.

2026
Submitted · Co-first author

Chaotic dynamics and control in a resonance-mediated acoustic droplet vaporization model

Tianye Zhang*, Ge Wang*, Chenzhe Wang, Jianyu Hua, and Chengzhi Shi

Submitted to Nonlinear Dynamics

Simulations reveal period-doubling and chaotic dynamics in a resonant liquid-droplet model. A weak second-harmonic field stabilizes a periodic trajectory while retaining a similar radial amplitude, motivating more predictable control of phase-change agents.

2026
Manuscript prepared for resubmission · First author

Weak-field regulation of phase-change nanodroplets enables acoustic nano-encoding

Tianye Zhang, Ge Wang, John Kim, and Chengzhi Shi

Manuscript in preparation

Hydrophone experiments show that weak auxiliary ultrasound can concentrate selected spectral components and reduce emission variability after droplet vaporization. Separate simulations explore switching these states for acoustic encoding, a potential route to identifiable biomedical readouts.
